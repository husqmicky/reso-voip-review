Reso VoipArchitecture
Section 06
Hybrid Mesh Architecture
  • China-server-based infrastructure
  • On mesh signal loss, switches to cellular instantly (and vice versa)
  • Appears to keep both connections (mesh · cellular) alive at once
  • Not a switch (X) → connect both, then select (O)
  • Can see the peer's state (whether mesh or Voip is active)
  • Benefits are maximized with the same model (Pilot · Air)

Reso VoipVoice Quality
Section 07
Voice Quality
  • Despite the physical disadvantage of a China server, perceived latency is excellent
  • Latency advantage maximized by skipping phone encode-decode
  • Reso's claim (150ms) looks somewhat exaggerated
  • 100ms+ advantage over other Voip
  • No notable quality difference between mesh and Voip states
